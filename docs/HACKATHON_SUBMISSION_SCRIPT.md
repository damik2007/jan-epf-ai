### **Project Title**: Jan-EPF AI — Rebuilding India's Provident Fund (EPFO)
### **Tagline**: Topic-Centric, Sovereign Digital Public Infrastructure for 70 Million Indian Citizens.

**The Problem**:  
India's EPFO manages \$250B+ for 70 million workers but suffers from a 48.4% claim rejection rate, 20-day clerical delays, and frequent portal crashes. Citizens are forced to navigate 18 cryptic form numbers (Form 31, 19, 10C, 13) and get rejected weeks later for single-letter name typos or blurry cheque uploads.

**The Solution**:  
Jan-EPF AI transforms EPFO into a consumer-first, zero-paper public good built around 4 natural life events: *I Need Money*, *I Changed Jobs*, *My Savings*, and *Fix My Details*.

**Key Breakthroughs**:

2. **80/20 Sovereign On-Device Core**: 80% of statutory rules execute client-side (<5ms, \$0 API bill for 70M citizens).
3. **1-Click Multi-Job Merge**: Auto-deduces missing Dates of Exit from ECR wage timestamps to consolidate past accounts in 1 tap.
4. **WCAG AAA Senior Mode**: Empathetic mode for 7.8M pensioners with 56px touch targets and voice-guided Jeevan Pramaan life certificate verification.
5. **Zero-Trust Privacy & SRE Resilience**: Presidio PII masking (DPDP Act 2023), PostgreSQL Row-Level Security, and a 6-circuit fault-tolerant matrix.
